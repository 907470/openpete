submodule: third_party/PsyCross (PsyCross, the cross-platform PSY-Q shim)
fork:      https://github.com/tyscorp/PsyCross.git — https://github.com/tyscorp/PsyCross
branch:    openpete
upstream:  https://github.com/OpenDriver2/PsyCross

The submodule tracks the `openpete` branch of the fork, not upstream. Every
patch in PATCHES/ is already a commit on that branch, so a fresh
`git submodule update --init` needs no patching; PATCHES/ is the reviewable
record of each change and its verification, and the fallback for a pin that
predates one. APPLIED_AT maps each patch to the fork commit that carries it.
tools/apply_third_party_patches.sh reconciles the two and fails when the pin
lacks a patch.
